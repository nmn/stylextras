## Accessibility and browser behavior

The menubar requires an accessible name. Left/Right moves among top-level menu
triggers with wrapping and respects RTL. When a submenu is open, that movement
switches to the adjacent submenu. Printable keys search the top-level trigger
labels. Pointer movement can switch an already-open menu without dispatching a
consumer click or stealing focus. Native `focusgroup` supplies roving focus
where supported and a feature-detected bridge fills the gap elsewhere.

## API reference

### Menubar

`MenubarProps`

Complete native/inherited prop base: `Omit<React.ComponentPropsWithRef<"div">, "aria-label" | "aria-labelledby" | "className" | "role" | "style">` plus one required accessible name.

- `aria-label` or `aria-labelledby` — Required accessible widget name.
- `sx` — `stylex.StyleXStyles`. Theme-aware local overrides composed after the built-in styles.
